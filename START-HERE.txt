LOYALTY ICT SOLUTIONS — QUICK START
====================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html          The whole app (store + admin in one file)
  manifest.json       Makes it installable as a phone app
  sw.js               Service worker (offline + install support)
  icon-192.png        App icon
  icon-512.png        App icon (large)
  FIREBASE-GUIDE.txt  How to turn this demo into a real business
  START-HERE.txt      This file

TRY IT NOW (on your computer)
  Just open index.html in Chrome. Browse the store, add items to
  cart, checkout (simulated M-Pesa), and get a printable receipt.

ACCOUNTS & ADMIN (new — no shared password)
  Click "Account" (top right) and REGISTER.
  - The FIRST person to register automatically becomes the ADMIN.
  - Everyone who registers after that is a normal customer.
  - The admin gets a "Team" tab where they assign roles to users:
        Customer = can shop only
        Staff    = manage products, orders and bookings
        Admin    = full access + can assign roles
  So you make yourself admin first, then promote your staff from
  inside the app. No password is stored anywhere in the code.

  As admin/staff you can: add/edit/delete products (with photos),
  approve orders, approve/reject service requests, print reports.

PUT IT ONLINE (free, 2 minutes)
  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag this WHOLE folder onto the page.
  3. Open the live link on your phone -> "Add to Home screen".

EDIT YOUR DETAILS
  - Phone number: it's set to 0713 474115 everywhere. Search the
    file for 254713474115 / 0713 474115 to change it.
  - Products: add your real ones through the Admin panel (with real
    photos and prices). The 20 sample products are just examples —
    delete them once yours are in.
  - Admin access: there is no password to change — the first
    person to register becomes admin. For real secure logins
    across devices, follow HOW-TO-USE-FIREBASE.md.

IMPORTANT — READ FIREBASE-GUIDE.txt
  This demo saves data in the browser only. For customer orders to
  actually reach you, and to take real M-Pesa payments securely,
  follow the steps in that guide. It's written in plain language.
